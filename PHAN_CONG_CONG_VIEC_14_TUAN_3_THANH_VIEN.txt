PHAN CONG CONG VIEC DO AN 14 TUAN - 3 THANH VIEN
De tai: Xay dung he thong giam sat va bao dong an ninh thong minh (Security Monitoring System)

PHAN VAI THANH VIEN
- Thanh vien 1 - Backend/Database: FastAPI, REST API, xac thuc, PostgreSQL, MQTT backend, Docker va tich hop he thong.
- Thanh vien 2 - Frontend: giao dien dashboard, cac man hinh quan ly, ket noi REST API/WebSocket va hien thi du lieu.
- Thanh vien 3 - AI/IoT: YOLOv8, xu ly camera/frame, ESP32, cam bien, firmware va giao tiep MQTT.
- Ca 3 cung tham gia phan tich, thong nhat API/topic/payload, tich hop, danh gia ket qua va viet bao cao. Phan cong nay la ke hoach du kien; dieu chinh theo tien do thuc te cua nhom.

KE HOACH THEO TUAN

Tuan 1 - Khoi dong va xac dinh yeu cau
TV1: Lap danh sach chuc nang backend, du kien mo hinh du lieu va API.
TV2: Khao sat doi tuong su dung, phac thao dashboard va luong thao tac.
TV3: Khao sat camera, YOLO, ESP32 va cac cam bien co the dung.
Ca nhom: Chot muc tieu, pham vi, vai tro va ke hoach 14 tuan.
Dau ra: De cuong, yeu cau chuc nang/phi chuc nang, lich cong viec.

Tuan 2 - Phan tich nghiep vu va kien truc
TV1: Thiet ke kien truc backend, database va phan quyen.
TV2: Ve wireframe cac man hinh Tong quan, Camera, Canh bao, IoT va Lich su.
TV3: Chon phuong an nhan dien, luong xu ly frame va danh sach phan cung.
Ca nhom: Thong nhat contract REST, WebSocket va MQTT.
Dau ra: So do kien truc, ERD ban dau, wireframe, API/topic contract.

Tuan 3 - Thiet ke chi tiet va chuan bi moi truong
TV1: Tao project FastAPI, cau hinh SQLAlchemy/PostgreSQL, cau truc module.
TV2: Tao bo khung frontend, quy uoc component, mau sac va responsive.
TV3: Cai dat moi truong AI, thu model YOLO; lap so do dau noi ESP32/cam bien.
Ca nhom: Tao repository, quy uoc branch/commit, bien moi truong va huong dan chay.
Dau ra: Bo khung chay duoc, tai lieu thiet lap moi truong.

Tuan 4 - Database va giao dien tong quan
TV1: Tao bang User, Camera, Device, Detection, Alert, Sensor va log.
TV2: Dung layout dashboard, sidebar, header, the thong ke va bieu do.
TV3: Thu doc camera/frame va chay inference YOLO tren anh mau.
Ca nhom: Review schema va giao dien voi du lieu mau.
Dau ra: Database schema ban dau, dashboard prototype, ket qua thu YOLO.

Tuan 5 - Xac thuc va quan ly camera
TV1: Lam dang nhap JWT, API nguoi dung va CRUD camera.
TV2: Lam man hinh dang nhap, danh sach camera, tim kiem/loc va form them sua.
TV3: Chuan hoa camera_id, thu RTSP va quy trinh lay frame.
Ca nhom: Chot cach bao mat thong tin RTSP va mapping camera.
Dau ra: Dang nhap va quan ly camera co the tich hop.

Tuan 6 - Su kien AI va trang giam sat
TV1: Lam API tiep nhan detection, luu su kien va truy van lich su.

Tuan 7 - Canh bao va thong ke

Tuan 8 - ESP32 va thu thap du lieu IoT

- Thanh vien 1: backend/app, database, API, auth, MQTT adapter, Docker va tai lieu API.
- Thanh vien 2: frontend, dashboard, cac trang quan ly, ket noi REST/WebSocket va tai lieu giao dien.
- Thanh vien 3: ai-service, YOLO/model, firmware ESP32, cam bien va tai lieu IoT.

Luu y: Tuan va dau ra tren la ke hoach tham khao. Nhom can ghi nhan cong viec da lam theo thuc te, khong dung ke hoach nay de khang dinh cac hang muc da hoan thanh.
